Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Summary

Your privacy is important to us. This app does not require an account and does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information to the developer.

The app uses Google AdMob to support development through advertising, and Google may process certain device, advertising, diagnostic, and interaction information in connection with providing ads.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use this application (the “App”).

The App is designed to operate primarily on your device. The developer does not operate a user account system, does not maintain a database of user profiles, and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, or the content you create with the App.

The App is supported in part through advertising provided by Google AdMob. Because AdMob is a third-party service, certain information may be processed by Google when advertisements are requested, displayed, or interacted with.

2. Information Collected by the Developer

The developer does not directly collect or store personal information through the App.

In particular, the App does not require you to:

  • Create an account;
  • Provide your name or email address;
  • Provide a phone number;
  • Upload personal information to a developer-operated server; or
  • Submit the content you create to the developer.

Content created using the App is processed locally on your device unless you independently choose to save, export, or share it using features provided by your device or other services.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements and help support the continued development of the App.

The use of advertisements is not intended to require you to disclose information directly to the developer. Interacting with an advertisement is optional, and you are not required to click or otherwise engage with an advertisement in order to use the App.

However, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain information when advertising services are used. Depending on your device, region, privacy settings, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location;
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers where permitted;
  • Information about advertisements shown to you;
  • Interactions with advertisements or advertising features;
  • App interaction information;
  • Performance information;
  • Diagnostic information, including certain crash-related information; and
  • Other technical information used for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, or fraud prevention.

This information is processed by Google under Google's own privacy practices. The developer does not receive a personally identifiable profile of individual users from AdMob.

4. Tracking and Advertising Choices

Where required by Apple policies or applicable law, the App will respect the privacy choices and permissions available through iOS.

If the App requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, you may choose whether to allow tracking. Declining such permission does not prevent you from using the core functionality of the App.

You may also manage relevant privacy, tracking, and advertising settings through your device settings.

The availability and behavior of personalized or non-personalized advertisements may vary depending on your region, device configuration, consent choices, and Google AdMob requirements.

5. Photos and Content Created by You

If the App allows you to create, save, or export images, Live Photos, videos, or other content, that content is handled on your device unless you choose to share it.

The developer does not automatically upload your photos, generated graphics, videos, Live Photos, or other created content to a developer-operated server.

When you choose to save content to your photo library, the App may request the appropriate system permission from iOS. Such access is used only to perform the function you requested.

If you choose to share content using the iOS sharing interface or another application, the information you share will then be handled according to the privacy practices of the service or application you select.

6. Third-Party Services

The primary third-party service integrated into the App is Google AdMob.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, technical requirements, and legal obligations. Their practices are controlled by the respective third-party providers and may change independently of this App.

The developer seeks to use established third-party services that provide appropriate privacy protections, but cannot control the independent data-processing practices of those providers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer does not directly collect or maintain personal user information through the App, there is generally no developer-operated user database containing personal information that needs to be deleted upon request.

Content that you create and save locally remains under your control and may be deleted through the App, your photo library, or the relevant system features, as applicable.

Information processed independently by Google through AdMob is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

If you believe that you have provided information directly to the developer, for example through a voluntary support or feedback email, you may contact the developer to request deletion of that correspondence or associated information where applicable.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but reasonable efforts are made to use reputable platform services and third-party components.
