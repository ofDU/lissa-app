Jumbotron example

Custom jumbotron

Using a series of utilities, you can create this jumbotron, just like the one in previous versions of Bootstrap. Check out the examples below for how you can remix and restyle it to your liking.

Open the app

Please go to the app view on your Apple Watch, then tap this app.

Switch between views

Swipe left or right to switch between views. Each view lets you adjust a different aspect of the graphic.

Edit the graphic

Use the Digital Crown to adjust the graphic. Rotate the Crown, and the graphic in the current view will change accordingly. Combine view switching with Digital Crown rotation to create your own unique graphics.

Goal

Use small, precise rotations to make stable and simple graphics. The controls can be sensitive in certain aspects.

Create Live Photo

Create a Live Photo from the graphic you made and share it with friends!

Change the background

Swap the background-color utility and add a `.text-*` color utility to mix up the jumbotron look. Then, mix and match with additional component themes and more.
